Zerzura Data Use Undertaking
Version 1.0

DRAFT. This text has not been reviewed by counsel. Read it, change it, and make
it your own before serving anything that matters.

This undertaking is given to the town "zerzura" by the person identified as the
subject of the credential presented alongside it. It is a direct undertaking to
this town. It neither replaces nor modifies any agreement the undersigned holds
with any other party, and no such agreement is required to be disclosed to this
town.

By signing the digest of this document with the private key bound to that
credential subject, I affirm that:

1. I hold credentialed access to PhysioNet and have signed the PhysioNet
   Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement, version 1.5.0.

2. I extend to any result I receive from this town the same undertakings I gave
   in that agreement. In particular:

   a. I will not attempt to identify any individual or institution referenced
      in, or derivable from, those results.
   b. I will not share access to those results with anyone who has not given
      this same undertaking.
   c. I will maintain the physical and electronic security of those results.
   d. I will use them solely for lawful scientific research.

3. I will promptly report any apparent identifying information to this town's
   operator, and will not further examine or distribute it.

4. I understand that results from this town are aggregate statistics protected
   by minimum-cell-size suppression; that suppression is not a formal privacy
   guarantee; and that deliberately composing multiple queries in order to
   isolate an individual or a small group would breach paragraph 2(a), whether
   or not any single query is refused.

5. I understand that this undertaking is recorded in this town's local audit
   log, together with my credential subject identifier and the time of signing,
   and that my requests are recorded there too.

These obligations continue after I stop requesting results from this town.
