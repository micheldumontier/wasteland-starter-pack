MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo, version 2.2
============================================

This directory contains three tables from the MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo,
redistributed verbatim: hosp/patients, hosp/admissions and icu/icustays. They
are the only tables this town's service reads. The other 23 tables of the demo,
and the full MIMIC-IV database, are not included.

These are real, de-identified records from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center.
The demo is open access: no credentialed PhysioNet account and no data use
agreement are required to obtain it. They are bundled here so that this
repository's evidence document can run against real data from a clean checkout,
with no network access.
